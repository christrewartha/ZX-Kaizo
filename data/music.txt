; The soundtrack: three voices, written as a step grid like a tracker.
;
;   tick <frames>                    frames per step (50 frames a second)
;   instrument <A|B|C> <volume> <floor> <fade>
;                                    a note starts at volume (0-15) and
;                                    drops by one every <fade> frames until
;                                    it reaches floor
;   A: <steps...>                    steps for a voice; lines add up
;
; A step is a note (C1 to B7, with # or b, e.g. F#3 Bb4), '-' to hold the
; previous note, or '.' for silence. '|' is ignored: use it for bar lines.
; All three voices must be the same length; the song loops forever.
;
; The AY on 128K machines (or a 48K AY interface) plays it. A plain 48K is
; silent.

tick 9                          ; a 16th note: 83 beats per minute

instrument A 12 7 6             ; bass: soft, held
instrument B 10 3 3             ; electric piano: plucked, fades quickly
instrument C 11 5 5             ; vibes: rings for a while
