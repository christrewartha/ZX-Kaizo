; The world: every room, laid out on a grid exactly as they connect.
;
; Rooms are declared first:
;   room <x> <y> <name>              grid position (0,0 = top left) and the
;                                    name shown under the play area
;   enemy <walker|flyer> <x> <y> <min> <max> <speed px/frame> <colour>
;                                    belongs to the room above it; walkers
;                                    patrol x between min and max, flyers y
; Colours: blue red magenta green cyan yellow white
;
; After "map:" comes the grid. Each room is 32 columns x 22 rows; rooms in
; a grid row are separated by '|', and grid rows by one line of '-'. Grid
; slots with no room are filled with '~'. Trailing spaces may be trimmed.
;   ' ' empty   '#' brick   'X' block   '=' one-way platform
;   '^' floor spikes   'v' ceiling spikes   'S' player start (one, anywhere)
;
; Exits come from the grid: walk off any edge that has a room beside it.
; Falling off the bottom with no room below is fatal; the top of the world
; is an invisible ceiling. tools/build_data.ps1 warns about doorways that
; don't line up with the room next door.
;
; Design rules of thumb:
; - A ground jump rises 34 px, so a platform can be at most 4 rows above
;   the surface you jump from, and a gap at most ~5 columns wide.
; - Doorways between rooms are rows 16-19, with the floor at rows 20-21.
; - To jump UP into the room above: a platform at row 3 (or higher) under a
;   ceiling hole, and a one-way '=' across the hole in the floor above.
;   A plain hole in the floor is a one-way drop.
