How to play

Climb, fall and wall-jump through 15 levels of a haunted castle, past spikes, beetles and bats. Each level is one screen: reach the flashing exit in the right-hand wall. Lives are unlimited.

Each level also hides a trophy, off the way to the exit and harder to get to. Grab it, then reach the exit to win it. Die on the way and it goes back.

Press Space at the start screen to begin. Click the Spectrum's screen first if it isn't taking your keys.

Use the buttons under the screen: START at the start screen, the arrows to run, JUMP to jump, hold DROP to fall through a platform, and SKIP to give up on a level and go to the next. Slide your thumb across the arrows to change direction. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger screen.

Play it on a real Spectrum

Download the tape image, kaizxo.tap, for any emulator, or for a TZXDuino or DivMMC. It runs on any 48K or 128K Spectrum. The music needs a 128K, or a 48K with an AY interface.

There's also a Commodore 64 version, Kaiz64o, with the same levels.
