KaiZXo

A platformer with no mercy.

How to play

Climb, fall and wall-jump through 15 levels of a haunted castle, past spikes, beetles and bats. Each level is one screen: reach the flashing exit in the right-hand wall. Lives are unlimited.

Left / rightO P
JumpQ, Space or M
Drop through a platformhold A

Press Space at the start screen to begin. Click the Spectrum's screen first if it isn't taking your keys. You need a keyboard to play: phones and tablets aren't supported yet.

Play it on a real Spectrum

Download the tape image, kaizxo.tap, for any emulator, or for a TZXDuino or DivMMC. It runs on any 48K or 128K Spectrum. The music needs a 128K, or a 48K with an AY interface.
