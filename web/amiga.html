How to play

The Amiga takes about 20 seconds to start up. Then each level opens with a fly-over from the exit back to the start: the levels are bigger than the screen now.

Climb, fall and wall-jump as Foxy through 15 levels, down in the old ruins and out over the hills, past spikes, opossums and eagles. One touch of any of them and it's back to the start. Reach the door. Lives are unlimited.

Each level also hides a gem, off the way to the door and harder to get to. Grab it, then reach the door to keep it. Die on the way and it goes back.

Press Space at the title screen to begin, or to skip a fly-over. Click the Amiga's screen first if it isn't taking your keys.

Use the buttons under the screen: START at the title screen, the arrows to run, JUMP to jump and hold DROP to fall through a platform. Slide your thumb across the arrows to change direction. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger screen.

Play it on a real Amiga

Download the disk image, kaizamigo.adf, for any emulator, or for a real Amiga with a Gotek. It boots from the disk and takes over the machine; so far it has been tested in emulation, on an A500 with the free AROS ROM. Use a joystick in port 2.

The same castle on the other machines: KaiZXo for the ZX Spectrum and Kaiz64o for the Commodore 64.
