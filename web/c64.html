How to play

Climb, fall and wall-jump through 15 levels of a haunted castle, past spikes, beetles and bats. Each level is one screen: reach the exit in the right-hand wall. Lives are unlimited.

Each level also hides a trophy, off the way to the exit and harder to get to. Grab it, then reach the exit to win it. Die on the way and it goes back.

Press Space at the title screen to begin. Click the C64's screen first if it isn't taking your keys.

Use the buttons under the screen: START at the title screen, the arrows to run, JUMP to jump, hold DROP to fall through a platform, and SKIP to give up on a level and go to the next. Slide your thumb across the arrows to change direction. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger screen.

Play it on a real C64

Download the disk image, kaiz64o.d64, for any emulator, or for a real C64 with an SD2IEC, Pi1541 or 1541 Ultimate. Type LOAD "*",8,1, then RUN. Use a joystick in either port, or the keys above (O, P, Q, A, Space, M, N).

The same castle on other machines: KaiZXo for the ZX Spectrum, and Kaizamigo for the Amiga.
