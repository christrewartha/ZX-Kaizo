; Sprite art for Kaiz64o, the C64 version. tools/build_c64.ps1 packs it
; into build/c64gen/sprite_data.c as SP_<NAME> blocks.
;
; Multicolour sprites: each pixel is two screen pixels wide.
;   '.' clear   'k' dark grey (shared)   'w' white (shared)   'o' own colour
; Up to 19 rows of up to 10 pixels. The first 8 columns and 16 rows are
; the 16x16 box the game logic uses, as on the Spectrum; S marks in
; data/world.txt and all the collision boxes are relative to its top-left.
;
; [name] mirror    also a left-facing copy, SP_<NAME>_L
; [name] outline   also a hi-res black outline traced round the art,
;                  SP_<NAME>_O, laid over it with a second sprite
